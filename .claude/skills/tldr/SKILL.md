# Explain in simple terms

- You have just said a long piece of text in the chat.
- You need to summarise the text into a few short bullet points.
- Please use simple language, around 16 years reading age.
- Start each bullet point with a bold short statement that summarises the main idea. The point of this is so the human reader can get a quick idea of what is being said and decide if any further reading is necessary.
- After this bold statement, provide a brief explanation or supporting detail in simple language.
- Presume the human will ask for clarification or more details about any of the bullet points if they so wish

Your job with this prompt is to make it very quick and easy for the human reader to grasp the main points of the text.
