# Download your passport

Your passport belongs to you. You can take all of it with you whenever
you like, and what you download does not depend on Quill.

## Download it

1. Choose [**Passport**](/passport), then **Open download**, or go straight to [**Download**](/passport/download).
   ![The Download page, with its three formats](download-your-passport/download.png)
2. Press the format you want.

## Which format

- **PDF** is the one to print or to send to somebody.
- **Markdown** is plain text, readable anywhere.
- **Full bundle** is everything: the record itself, both of the above, and its whole history, with a page explaining how to check that nothing has been altered.

You can download your passport even when it is read-only.
