# Add somebody to the passport

Anybody who holds a clinical profession on Quill can already use the
passport where it is switched on. This guide is for giving it to somebody
who has no account yet, or who needs the passport and nothing else.

## Add a new person

1. In the side menu, choose [**Admin**](/admin), then [**Users**](/admin/users).
2. Press the **Add user** button, beside the search box above the list. It is an icon with no words.
3. On **Basic details**, enter their name, email, username and an initial password. For **Base profession**, choose **Passport delegate**.
   ![The basic details step, with Passport delegate chosen](add-somebody-to-the-passport/basic-details.png)
4. On **Organisation/site**, choose where they work.
5. On **Competencies**, under **Held beyond the profession**, add **Hold and Write a Clinician Passport**. Without it they can read a passport and cannot start or add to one.
6. Press **Next** through the remaining steps, and on **Review** press **Create user**.

Quill does not email them by itself. Give them their username and
initial password, or open their page from [**Users**](/admin/users) and
press **Send** on the **Send invite email** card.

## The four passport professions

- **Passport delegate** is somebody who keeps a passport.
- **Passport clinical lead** is a label for the clinician who leads the passport where they work. It gives nothing more.
- **Passport admin** can add people and give them these professions, as you can. An admin can also add to a passport of their own.
- **Passport external assessor** is for an assessor from another organisation, who signs and does nothing else.

## Where everybody is covered

An organisation can pay for all of its staff and trainees to add to their
passports. Where it does, step 5 is not needed for them. Somebody who
operates Quill switches that on, on the organisation's **Features** page,
under **Clinician passport**.
