# Change a safety officer

This is a preview of safety cases in Quill. The five cases are examples, and a change you make lasts until the page is reloaded.

A safety case names the people answerable for it. The standards ask for
a clinical safety officer who is a registered clinician, and Quill keeps
their name with the case, beside their deputy, the product owner and top
management.

## See who holds each role

1. Choose [**Safety**](/safety) in the side menu, and open a case.
2. On the **Officers** card, press **Open officers**.
   ![The four officers of a safety case](change-a-safety-officer/officers.png)
3. There is a card for each of the four roles, with the holder's name and email address.

## Change who holds a role

1. Press the pencil on the role's card.
   ![Changing the holder of a role](change-a-safety-officer/edit.png)
2. Enter the new holder's **Name** and **Email**. The role itself cannot be changed.
3. Press **Save changes**. The card shows the new holder.

**Save changes** cannot be pressed until there is a name and an email
address that looks like one.
