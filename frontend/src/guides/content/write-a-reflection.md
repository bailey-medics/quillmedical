# Write a reflection

A reflection is what you took from a case, a complaint or a significant
event. It sits in your passport beside the record it relates to.

## Write one

1. Choose [**Passport**](/passport), then **Open reflections**, or go straight to [**Reflections**](/passport/reflections).
   ![The Reflections page](write-a-reflection/reflections.png)
2. Press **Write a reflection**.
3. Give it a **Title** you would find it by, and the day it was **Written on**.
   ![The form for a reflection, with its anonymity check](write-a-reflection/reflection.png)
4. Under **Your reflection**, write what happened, what you took from it, and what you would do differently.
5. Tick **I confirm this reflection is anonymised**.
6. Press **Save reflection**.

## Keep it anonymous

A reflection must hold nothing that could identify a patient: no name,
no date of birth, no NHS or hospital number, and no detail so unusual
that it would single somebody out. Write about the work and about you.

You are asked to confirm this each time you save, including when you
change a reflection you have already written.

## Read and change it

Choose a reflection to open it, and press **Edit reflection** to change
it.
