# Keep your logbook

Your logbook is your own record of the procedures you have performed.
Nobody signs it: it is what you say you did. Each entry counts towards a
competency, so the logbook is the evidence an assessor looks at before
they sign you off.

## Add an entry

1. Choose [**Passport**](/passport), then **Open logbook**, or go straight to [**Logbook**](/passport/logbook).
   ![The logbook, with entries grouped by competency](keep-your-logbook/logbook.png)
2. Press **Add an entry**.
3. Under **Which competency?**, choose the one this procedure counts towards.
4. Under **Performed on**, give the day it happened.
   ![The form for a logbook entry](keep-your-logbook/entry.png)
5. Fill in what is useful: **Setting**, **Supervision**, **Supervisor**, **Indication**, **Outcome** and **Notes**. All are optional.
6. Press **Add entry**.

Write about the procedure, never about a patient. Leave out anything
that could identify somebody.

## Read and correct it

Entries are grouped by competency, each with a count. Choose an entry to
open it, and press **Edit entry** to correct a mistake.
