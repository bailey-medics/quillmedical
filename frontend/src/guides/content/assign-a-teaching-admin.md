# Assign a teaching admin

A teaching admin adds delegates and sees their results, at the
organisations they belong to. Making somebody one takes two things: the
**Teaching admin** profession, and membership of an organisation that has
teaching switched on.

## Check the organisation has teaching switched on

1. In the side menu, choose [**Admin**](/admin), then [**Organisations**](/admin/organisations), and open the organisation.
2. Look at the **Enabled features** card. If **Teaching** is listed, go to the next section.
   ![An organisation's page, with its Enabled features card](assign-a-teaching-admin/organisation.png)
3. If it is not, press the **Edit features** button on that card. It is an icon with no words.
4. Switch on **Teaching**, press **Save changes**, then press **Confirm**.
   ![The Features page, with Teaching switched on](assign-a-teaching-admin/features.png)

Teaching is switched on for a whole organisation. It cannot be switched
on for one site.

## Give a new person the role

1. On the organisation's page, press the **Add staff** button :icon-user-plus:, above the organisation's staff table.
2. Under **Username or email address**, type their email address and press **Find**.
3. Quill says **Nobody on Quill uses** that address. Press **Create new user**. If it says they already have a Quill account, go to the next section instead.
   ![The organisation's Add staff page, offering to create a new user](assign-a-teaching-admin/not-found.png)
4. The **Create new user** form opens with their email address and the organisation already filled in. On **Basic details**, enter their name, username and an initial password. For **Base profession**, choose **Teaching admin**.
   ![The basic details step, with Teaching admin chosen](assign-a-teaching-admin/basic-details.png)
5. On **Organisation/site**, check their organisation is chosen under **Organisation**. A teaching admin can only act where they belong.
6. Press **Next** through **Competencies** and **Practice** without changing anything.
7. On **Enrolment**, tick a module only if they are to take it themselves. A teaching admin does not need to be enrolled to add delegates or see results.
8. On **Platform role**, leave **Standard** chosen.
9. On **Review**, check the details and press **Create user**.

## Give somebody who already has an account the role

1. Choose [**Admin**](/admin), then [**Users**](/admin/users), and choose the person.
2. On the **Edit user** card, press **Edit**.
3. On **Basic details**, change **Base profession** to **Teaching admin**.
4. On **Organisation/site**, check their organisation is chosen.
5. Press **Next** to the last step and save.

## Tell them

Quill does not email them by itself. Give them their username and
initial password, or open their page from [**Users**](/admin/users) and press **Send** on
the **Send invite email** card.

![A user's page, with the Edit user and Send invite email cards](assign-a-teaching-admin/user.png)

When they sign in they have an **Admin** entry in the side menu, and the
guides written for admins under [**Guides**](/guides).

## Another way

You can also create a new teaching admin from the list of users and choose their organisation yourself. See [Create a teaching admin from the users list](/guides/create-a-teaching-admin-from-the-users-list).
