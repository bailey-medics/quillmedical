# Read an incident report

This is a preview of safety cases in Quill. The five cases are examples, and a change you make lasts until the page is reloaded.

An incident is something that went wrong while the system was in use.
Each one is tied to the hazard it realised, so an incident is evidence
that a hazard is real and a test of whether its safeguards held.

## Find the incidents

1. Choose [**Safety**](/safety) in the side menu, and open a case.
2. On the **Incidents** card, press **Open incidents**.
   ![The incidents reported against a safety case](read-an-incident-report/incidents.png)
3. The newest is first. **Severity** is **Low**, **Moderate** or **High**, and **Hazard** names the hazard the incident belongs to.

## Read a report

1. Choose an incident from the list.
   ![One incident report](read-an-incident-report/incident.png)
2. **What happened** gives the account, when it occurred and the role of whoever reported it. A report names a role and never a person.
3. **Hazard realised** is a link to the hazard. Follow it to see the risk and what is being done about it.
4. **Immediate action**, **Root cause** and **Outcome** say what was done at the time, why it happened and how it ended.

You can also reach an incident from its hazard: open the hazard, and its
incidents are listed at the bottom of the page.
