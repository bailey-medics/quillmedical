# Create a clinical lead

Every site has should have a clinical lead. Quill emails them a certificate when a delegate at their site passes, and delegates who [join a course](/guides/join-a-course) find their site by typing its clinical lead's email address. Give the clinical lead an account first, then name them as the lead of their site.

## Before you start

Have these to hand:

- the clinical lead's **full name** and **email address**.
- a **username** for them
- a first **password** of at least 8 characters

## Open the new user form

1. In the side menu, choose [**Admin**](/admin), then [**Users**](/admin/users).
2. Press the **Add user** button, above the list of users. It is an icon of a person with a plus sign.
3. The [**Create new user**](/admin/users/new) page opens.

## Fill in the six steps

Press **Next** to move on from each step.

1. **Basic details.** Enter the full name, email, username and initial password. For **Base profession**, choose **Teaching clinical lead**.
   ![The basic details step, filled in for a clinical lead](create-a-clinical-lead/basic-details.png)
2. **Organisation/site.** If their site has been added already, choose it under **Site**. If it has not been created, choose your organisation under **Organisation** instead. The account is not created unless you choose at least one.
   ![The organisation and site step, with the organisation chosen](create-a-clinical-lead/organisation-site.png)
3. **Competencies.** Leave both boxes empty.
4. **Practice.** Leave the switches as they are. A clinical lead does not need them to be sent certificates.
5. **Enrolment.** Leave every module unticked, unless they are to take modules themselves.
6. **Review.** Check the details, then press **Create user**.
   ![The review step, with the Create user button](create-a-clinical-lead/review.png)

You are taken back to the list of users, with the message **User created**.

## Name them as the site's clinical lead

- **If the site has not been added yet**, [add the site](/guides/add-a-site) and choose them under **Clinical lead**.
- **If the site already exists**, open it from [**Sites**](/admin/sites), press the **Edit site** icon, and choose them under **Clinical lead**.

Either way, they are added to the site as staff at the same time, if they are not there already.
