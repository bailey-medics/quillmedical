# Record CPD

Your passport totals your continuing professional development over each
appraisal year, so the figure your appraiser asks for is already worked
out.

## Record an activity

1. Choose [**Passport**](/passport), then **Open CPD**, or go straight to [**CPD**](/passport/cpd).
   ![The CPD page, totalled for one date range](record-cpd/cpd.png)
2. Press **Add an activity**.
3. Enter the **Date** it happened, and under **What was it?** describe it as you would to an appraiser.
   ![The form for a CPD activity](record-cpd/activity.png)
4. Choose its **Type**: conference, grand round, teaching day, course or other.
5. Enter **Points** if you are claiming any. One point is one hour.
6. Press **Record activity**.

## Read your totals

Under **Date range**, choose the year you want. The card shows the points
and the number of activities in it, then each activity.

## Match your own appraisal year

Until you say otherwise, a year runs June to June. If yours does not:

1. Choose [**Settings**](/settings), and on the **Clinician passport** card press **CPD date ranges**. You can also go straight to [**CPD date ranges**](/settings/cpd-date-ranges).
   ![The CPD date ranges page](record-cpd/date-ranges.png)
2. Press **Add a date range**.
3. Enter the first and last day of your appraisal year under **From** and **To**, and press **Add date range**.

Your CPD is then totalled over your own dates.
