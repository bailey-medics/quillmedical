# Read and edit a safety document

This is a preview of safety cases in Quill. The five cases are examples, and a change you make lasts until the page is reloaded.

Every safety case has four documents, and they are the evidence an
assessor reads. Quill keeps them with the case and fills in the details
that change, such as the product's version, so the four never disagree.

## Find the documents

1. Choose [**Safety**](/safety) in the side menu, and open a case.
2. On the **Documentation** card, press **Open documentation**.
   ![The four documents of a safety case](read-and-edit-a-safety-document/documents.png)
3. Each document shows its **Version** and whether it is **Approved** or **Draft**.

## Read a document

1. Choose a document from the list. It opens laid out as a page.
   ![A clinical risk management plan, laid out as a page](read-and-edit-a-safety-document/document.png)
2. The top of the page gives the system, the version and when the document was last updated. The sections follow.

The hazard log document is written from the case's own hazards, one
section for each, so it always matches the **Hazards** part of the case.

## Edit a document

1. With the document open, press **Edit document**.
   ![Editing a document as text](read-and-edit-a-safety-document/edit.png)
2. Change the text under **Document**. It is written in Markdown: a line starting with a hash is a heading, and a line starting with a dash is a bullet.
3. Press **Save changes**. You are taken back to the document, showing what you wrote.

**Save changes** cannot be pressed while the box is empty.

## Values that fill themselves in

Some words in a document are written inside double curly brackets, such
as the product's name. These are placeholders. When the document is
shown, each is replaced by its value from the **Placeholders** part of
the case, so a product name or version changes in every document at once.
Leave them as they are when you edit.
