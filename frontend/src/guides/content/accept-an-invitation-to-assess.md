# Accept an invitation to assess

A colleague has asked you to assess one of their competencies and record
the outcome in their clinician passport. A passport is a record of what a
clinician has been assessed as able to do, signed by whoever assessed
them.

## Open the invitation

1. Open the email. Its subject says who has asked you.
   ![The invitation email, with the Accept the invitation button](accept-an-invitation-to-assess/email.png)
2. Press **Accept the invitation**. The link works until you accept, and for 14 days.

If you were not expecting it, ignore the email and nothing happens.

## If you already use Quill

The page says there is nothing to set up. Press **Accept invitation**,
then sign in. The request is in your [**Inbox**](/inbox).

## If you are new to Quill

You register first, so that every sign-off you make is under your name
and your registration.

1. Enter **Your full name**, as it should read on the sign-offs you make.
2. Choose your **Registering body** and enter your **Registration number**. They are recorded on every sign-off you make.
3. Choose a username and a password.
4. Press **Accept invitation**. You are taken to sign in.

Your registration is recorded as you declare it. Quill does not check it
against the register.

## Then

Sign in on the [sign-in page](/login). The request is waiting in your
[**Inbox**](/inbox). The guide "Sign somebody off" takes you through it.
