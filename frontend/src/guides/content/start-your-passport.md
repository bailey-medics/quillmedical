# Start your passport

A clinician passport is your own record of what you have been assessed as
able to do, signed by the people who assessed you. It also holds your
logbook, your CPD, your certificates and your reflections. It belongs to
you, and you can download all of it at any time.

## Create it

1. In the side menu, choose [**Passport**](/passport). The page is called **My passport**.
   ![The My passport page before a passport has been created](start-your-passport/create.png)
2. Under **Your specialty**, choose one or more. Choose **Generic** if none fits. This only decides which competencies are listed first when you pick one, and you can change it later.
3. Press **Create my passport**. The page changes to your passport.

## Find your way round

![A passport, with a card for each of its parts and a list of records](start-your-passport/passport.png)

Your passport has a card for each of its six parts:

- **Sign-offs** is what an assessor has signed, and what is still waiting.
- **Logbook** is the procedures you have performed.
- **CPD** is your continuing professional development.
- **Certificates** is the courses, qualifications and awards you are claiming.
- **Reflections** is what you took from a case or an event.
- **Download** takes your whole record away with you.

Below the cards, **Records** lists everything in your passport, newest
first. Choose a row to open it.

## Change your specialty later

1. Choose [**Settings**](/settings) in the side menu.
2. On the **Clinician passport** card, change **Specialities**. It is saved as you change it.

## If your passport is read-only

A message at the top of the page says so. You can still read and download
everything. Adding to it needs your organisation, or you, to hold a
current entitlement, so ask whoever runs the passport where you work.
