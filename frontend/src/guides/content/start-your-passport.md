# Start your passport

A clinician passport is your own record of what you have been assessed as
able to do, signed by the people who assessed you. It also holds your
logbook, your CPD, your certificates and your reflections. It belongs to
you, and you can download all of it at any time.

## Create it

1. In the side menu, choose [**Passport**](/passport). The page is called **My passport**.
   ![The My passport page before a passport has been created](start-your-passport/create.png)
2. Under **Frameworks you work to**, choose one or more. A framework is a published set of competencies, such as a national curriculum or your own hospital's sign-off sheet. Type to find one by its name or its publisher, or choose a **Specialty** above to narrow the list. Your passport offers the competencies in the frameworks you choose, and you can change them later.
3. Press **Create my passport**. The page changes to your passport.

## Find your way round

![A passport, with a table of the competencies in a framework and a card for each of its other parts](start-your-passport/passport.png)

Your passport opens **By competency**. There is a table for each
framework you work to, with every competency in it:

- **Sign-off** says whether it is **Signed off**, **Requested** (waiting
  on an assessor), or **Not asked**.
- **Logbook** says how many entries count towards it.

Choose a row to open that competency. Its page shows its sign-offs and
its logbook, and has **Ask for a sign-off** and **Add an entry** buttons.

Below the tables are cards for the parts of your passport that belong to
no one competency: **CPD**, **Certificates**, **Reflections** and
**Download**.

## See your passport by record instead

![The same passport by record, with a card for each of its six parts and a list of records](start-your-passport/by-record.png)

**By record** has a card for each of the six parts of your passport,
**Sign-offs** and **Logbook** among them. Below the cards, **Records**
lists everything in your passport, newest first.

1. Choose [**Settings**](/settings) in the side menu.
2. On the **Clinician passport** card, under **Passport home page**, choose **By record**. It is saved as you choose it.

Choose **By competency** there to go back. You can change it on a
read-only passport too, because it changes only how your record is
shown.

## Change your frameworks later

1. Choose [**Settings**](/settings) in the side menu.
2. On the **Clinician passport** card, change **Frameworks you work to**. It is saved as you change it.

Add a framework when a competency you want is not offered: it belongs to a
framework you have not chosen. Removing a framework keeps everything you
have already recorded under it.

## If your passport is read-only

A message at the top of the page says so. You can still read and download
everything. Adding to it needs your organisation, or you, to hold a
current entitlement, so ask whoever runs the passport where you work.
