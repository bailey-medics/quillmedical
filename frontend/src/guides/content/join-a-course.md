# Join a course

You create your own account. It takes a few minutes, and you need three
things:

- your name as it should appear on your certificate
- an email address you can open now
- the email address of your clinical lead, who must already be on Quill

## Choose your module

1. Open the [register page](/register). You can also reach it from the sign-in page, by choosing **Don't have an account? Register**.
2. Under **Teaching module**, choose the module you are joining.
3. Under **Clinical lead email address**, type your clinical lead's email address. This is how Quill knows which site you belong to.
4. Press **Continue**.
   ![The first registration step, asking for a module and a clinical lead](join-a-course/choose-module.png)

If you see **Clinical lead not found**, your clinical lead is not on Quill
yet. Ask them, or whoever runs the course, to sort that out first.

## Create your account

1. Enter your **Full name**, as it should appear on certificates.
2. Choose a **Username** and enter your **Email**.
3. Choose a **Password** of at least 8 characters, and type it again under **Confirm password**.
4. Tick the box if you would rather not get news and updates.
5. Press **Register**.
   ![The form for creating an account](join-a-course/create-account.png)

## Confirm your email address

1. Open the email called **Verify your Quill email address** and press **Verify your email**. The link lasts 60 minutes. Look in your spam folder if it has not arrived.
2. When you see **Email verified**, choose **Go to login**.

If the link has run out, you see **Verification failed**. Choose **Sign
in for a new link** and sign in with your username and password. Quill
emails you a fresh link.

## Sign in

1. Enter your **Username** and **Password**, and press **Sign in**.
   ![The sign-in page](join-a-course/sign-in.png)
2. You arrive at **Teaching modules**. Press **View module** to begin.
