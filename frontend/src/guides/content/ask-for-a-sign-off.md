# Ask for a sign-off

A sign-off is somebody else's judgement that you can do something,
recorded in your passport under their name. You ask for one, and the
assessor you name decides.

## Ask

1. Choose [**Passport**](/passport), then on the **Sign-offs** card press **Open sign-offs**. You can also go straight to [**Sign-offs**](/passport/sign-offs).
   ![The Sign-offs page, with requests waiting and signed](ask-for-a-sign-off/sign-offs.png)
2. Press **Ask for a sign-off**.
3. Under **Which competency?**, find what you are asking to be signed off for. Type a word of it to search.
4. Under **Who should assess this?**, type your assessor's username or email address. A line beneath tells you whether they already use Quill. If they do not, they are emailed an invitation and can register to sign.
   ![The request form, with a competency and an assessor chosen](ask-for-a-sign-off/request.png)
5. Under **Observed on**, give the day the work happened, not today's date.
6. If the competency has levels, choose the one you are asking for. Your assessor may sign off a different level.
7. Add anything the assessor should know, and a reflection if you wish. Both are optional.
8. Press **Request sign-off**, check the name in the box that opens, and press **Send request**.

Your request appears under **Awaiting sign-off**, marked **Requested**.
Your assessor is emailed.

## See what happened

Choose a request to open it. Once it is signed, it shows who signed it,
on what basis, and their own notes.

![A signed sign-off, with who signed it and on what basis](ask-for-a-sign-off/signed.png)

An assessor's registration is shown as they declared it. Quill does not
check it against the register.

## Change your mind

Open a request that is still waiting and press **Withdraw request**. Your
assessor can no longer sign it, and you can ask again whenever you like.

## You cannot sign yourself off

A passport records a second person's judgement. Naming yourself as the
assessor is refused.
