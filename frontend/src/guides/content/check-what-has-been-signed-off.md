# Check what has been signed off

This is a preview of safety cases in Quill. The five cases are examples, and a change you make lasts until the page is reloaded.

A safety case is not finished until the right people have put their name
to it. Compliance sign-off shows each section that needs a signature,
who is to sign it, and whether they have. It answers "is this system
safe to go live?" on one page.

## See where a case stands

1. Choose [**Safety**](/safety) in the side menu, and open a case.
2. On the **Compliance sign-off** card, press **Open sign-off**. The card already says how many sections are signed.
   ![The sections of a safety case, signed and awaiting signature](check-what-has-been-signed-off/sections.png)
3. Each section shows who signs it. A tick and a date mean it is signed. A clock and **Awaiting signature** mean it is not.

A case has four sections: the clinical risk management plan, the hazard
log, the clinical safety case report, and top management approval.

## See what a signature covers

1. Choose a section.
   ![A section awaiting signature, with what it attests](check-what-has-been-signed-off/section.png)
2. **What this signature attests** says, in a sentence, what the signer is putting their name to.
3. **Documents reviewed** lists the documents the signature covers, each with its version. Choose one to read it.

A section that is waiting shows a **Record signature** button. In this
preview the button is there to show where signing will happen, and does
not record one yet.
