# Sign somebody off

When a colleague names you as their assessor, their request waits in
your inbox. Signing it puts your judgement, under your name, in their
passport.

## Find the request

1. Choose the envelope at the top of any page, or go to your [**Inbox**](/inbox). You are also emailed when a request arrives.
   ![The inbox, with a sign-off request waiting](sign-somebody-off/inbox.png)
2. Under **Waiting on you**, choose the request. It names who is asking and for which competency.

## Sign it

1. Read the request at the top of the page: the competency, the day it was observed, and anything your colleague wanted you to know.
   ![The sign-off page, with the request above the form](sign-somebody-off/sign-off.png)
2. Under **What did you do?**, choose **Directly observed**, **Reviewed evidence** or **Countersigned**. They are different acts, and the record says which.
3. If the competency has levels, check **Level**. It is set to what was asked for. Choose another if that is your judgement, and say why.
4. Add **Caveats** or **Assessment notes** if you wish.
5. Read the **Declaration** and tick it. You are confirming that you assessed this person, and that you accept professional accountability for the judgement.
6. Press **Sign off competency**.

You are taken back to your inbox, and the request moves to **Completed**.

## If you should not sign it

Press **Cancel** to leave it unsigned, and tell your colleague. They can
withdraw the request and ask somebody else.

You cannot sign off your own competency.
