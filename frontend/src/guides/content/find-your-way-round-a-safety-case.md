# Find your way round a safety case

This is a preview of safety cases in Quill. The five cases are examples, and a change you make lasts until the page is reloaded.

A safety case is the file of clinical safety evidence for one health IT
system. It holds the system's safety documents, its hazard log, the
incidents reported against it, the people responsible, and a record of
who has signed what off. Quill keeps all of it in one place, so a
clinical safety officer can see at a glance where a system stands.

## See every case

1. In the side menu, choose [**Safety**](/safety).
   ![The Safety page, listing every safety case](find-your-way-round-a-safety-case/cases.png)
2. Each row is one case. **Standard** says whether it is assessed against DCB0129 or DCB0160, **Open hazards** counts the hazards still open, and **Status** is **Draft**, **In review** or **Signed off**.
3. Press a column's heading to sort by it, or the **Search** icon above the list to find a case by its name, system or officer.

## Open a case

1. Choose a case from the list. Its page opens, with its status beside its name.
   ![A safety case, with a card for each of its six parts](find-your-way-round-a-safety-case/case.png)
2. The page has a card for each part of the case. Each card says how much is in that part, so the page is a summary of the whole case.

## The six parts

- **Documentation** holds the case's four documents: the clinical risk management plan, the hazard log, the clinical safety case report and the file index.
- **Hazards** is the hazard log: everything that could go wrong, how likely and how serious it is, and what has been done about it.
- **Incidents** lists what has gone wrong in use, each tied to the hazard it realised.
- **Officers** names the clinical safety officer, their deputy, the product owner and top management.
- **Compliance sign-off** shows which sections of the standard have been signed, and by whom.
- **Placeholders** holds values, such as the product's name and version, that are written once and filled into every document.

## Move about inside a case

The side menu shows where you are: **Safety**, then **Case**, then the
part you have open. Choose **Case** to go back to the six cards, or
**Safety** to go back to the list of cases.
