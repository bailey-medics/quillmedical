# Read the hazard log

This is a preview of safety cases in Quill. The five cases are examples, and a change you make lasts until the page is reloaded.

The hazard log is the heart of a safety case. It lists each way the
system could harm a patient, how likely and how serious that is, and
what has been done to make it safer. Quill shows the risk before and
after, so the effect of each safeguard is plain.

## Open the log

1. Choose [**Safety**](/safety) in the side menu, and open a case.
2. On the **Hazards** card, press **Open hazard log**.
   ![The hazard log of a safety case](read-the-hazard-log/hazards.png)

## Read the list

Each row is one hazard:

- **Hazard** says what could go wrong, **Cause** why, and **Effect** what it would mean for a patient.
- **Initial risk** is the risk before anything was done about it.
- **Residual risk** is the risk that is left now.
- **Status** is **Open**, **Mitigated** or **Closed**.

A risk is a number from 1 to 25, coloured by how serious it is. A hazard
whose two numbers are the same has not been made any safer yet.

## Open one hazard

1. Choose a hazard from the list.
   ![One hazard, with its risk before and after](read-the-hazard-log/hazard.png)
2. **Initial risk** and **Residual risk** sit side by side. Each shows how the number is reached: likelihood, from 1 to 5, times severity, from 1 to 5.
3. **Mitigation** says what was done to bring the risk down.
4. **Incidents** lists any time this hazard has happened in use. Choose one to read its report.

## What the numbers mean

- **1 to 2** is **Acceptable**.
- **3 to 4** is **Tolerable, review**.
- **5 to 9** is **Undesirable**.
- **10 and over** is **Unacceptable without mitigation**.
