# Add a certificate

Certificates are the courses, qualifications and awards you are claiming.
Nobody countersigns them. You can attach a scan or a photograph, so the
proof is with the claim.

## Record one

1. Choose [**Passport**](/passport), then **Open certificates**, or go straight to [**Certificates**](/passport/certificates).
   ![The Certificates page](add-a-certificate/certificates.png)
2. Press **Record a certificate**.
3. To attach the certificate itself, drop the file on the box at the top, or press the box to choose it. A PDF or a photograph will do.
   ![The form for a certificate, with the box for its file above it](add-a-certificate/certificate.png)
4. Under **What is it?**, give its name as the certificate has it, and under **Who issued it?**, the college, trust or provider.
5. Enter **Awarded on**, and **Expires on** if it has an end date.
6. Press **Record certificate**.

An expiry date is recorded, and nothing acts on it. What a lapsed
certificate means is for your appraiser to judge.

## Read and correct it

Choose a certificate to open it. Its file is shown beneath its details.
Press **Edit certificate** to change it or to attach a newer file.
