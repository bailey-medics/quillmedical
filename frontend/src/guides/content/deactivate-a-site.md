# Deactivate a site

Deactivate a site that is no longer taking part, for example one that has decided not to carry on with the course. Use this rather than deleting the site: its results are kept, and you can make it active again later.

## What deactivating does

- **The site stops taking part.** Nobody can register at it, and nobody can be added to it.
- **Some accounts are deactivated with it.** Anyone at the site who does not also belong to another site, or to the organisation's staff, can no longer sign in. Each of them is emailed to say their account has been closed.
- **Everyone else is left as they are.** A delegate who is also at another site, and anybody on the organisation's staff list, such as a teaching admin, keeps their account.
- **Results are kept.** Nothing anybody has sat is deleted.

Before you confirm, Quill tells you how many accounts will be deactivated.

## Deactivate the site

1. In the side menu, choose [**Admin**](/admin), then [**Sites**](/admin/sites).
2. Choose the site, then press **Edit**.
3. Below the site's details, find the **Deactivate site** card and press the red **Deactivate site** button.
4. Read the message. It says how many accounts will be deactivated with the site.
5. Press **Deactivate** to go ahead, or **Cancel** to leave the site as it is.

You are taken back to the site's page, which now shows it as **Deactivated**, with the message **Site deactivated** saying how many accounts were deactivated.

## Make a site active again

1. Open the site's edit page, as above.
2. In the **Reactivate site** card, press **Reactivate site**, then **Reactivate**.

People can be added to the site again. No account is brought back with it: reactivate each person you want back from their own page under [**Users**](/admin/users).
