# Deactivate a site

You can do this for example if a site no longer wishes to provide services as part of your organisation.

## What deactivating does

- **The site stops taking part.** Nobody can register at it, and nobody can be added to it.
- **Some accounts are deactivated with it.** Anyone at the site who does not also belong to another site, or to the organisation's staff, can no longer sign in. Each of them is emailed to say their account has been deactivated.
- **Everyone else is left as they are.** A delegate who is also at another site, and anybody on the organisation's staff list, such as a teaching admin, keeps their account.
- **Results are kept.** Assessment results are kept until the deletion internal has come about (normally 2 - 3.5 years later)

Before you confirm, Quill tells you how many accounts will be deactivated.

## Deactivate the site

1. In the side menu, choose [**Admin**](/admin), then [**Sites**](/admin/sites).
   ![The list of sites](deactivate-a-site/sites.png)
2. Choose the site, and on its page press the **Edit site** button :button-pencil: .
   ![The site's page, with the Edit site button](deactivate-a-site/site.png)
3. Below the site's details, find the **Deactivate site** card and press the red **Deactivate site** button.
   ![The site's edit page, with the Deactivate site card](deactivate-a-site/edit.png)
4. Read the message. It says how many accounts will be deactivated with the site.
   ![The message asking you to confirm, with the number of accounts](deactivate-a-site/confirm.png)
5. Press **Deactivate** to go ahead.

You are taken back to the site's page, which now shows it as **Deactivated**, with the message **Site deactivated** saying how many accounts were deactivated.

![The site's page after deactivating, with the Site deactivated message](deactivate-a-site/deactivated.png)

## Make a site active again

1. Open the site's edit page, as above.
2. In the **Reactivate site** card, press **Reactivate site**.

After the above, people can be added to the site again. Deactivated accounts are not automatically reactivated however. You will need to reactivate each person from their own page under [**Users**](/admin/users).
