# Take a module and its assessment

A module has two parts: learning materials to read at your own pace, and
an assessment. You can leave the learning materials and come back. The
assessment is sat in one go.

## Open your module

1. Sign in. You arrive at **Teaching modules**. If you are somewhere else, choose **Teaching** in the side menu.
   ![The Teaching modules page, with a card for each module](take-a-module-and-its-assessment/teaching-modules.png)
2. Press **View module** on the module you want.
   ![A module's page, with its learning materials and its assessment](take-a-module-and-its-assessment/module.png)

## Work through the learning materials

Some modules have no learning materials. If yours has none, go straight
to the assessment.

1. Press **Start learning**.
2. Press **Next** to move through the slides. The arrow keys work too, and on a phone you can swipe.
3. On the last slide, press **Finish**. You are taken back to the module.

To stop part way, choose **Exit lesson** in the side menu. Your place is
kept.

## Sit the assessment

Pick a time when you will not be interrupted. Once you begin, leaving the
page hands in whatever you have answered.

1. Press **Start assessment**.
2. If the module has an introduction, read it, then press **Begin**.
3. Answer each question and press **Next**. You can go back and change an answer.
4. On the last question, press **Submit & finish**.
5. Press **View results**.

If the module is timed, a countdown shows at the top of the page. When it
reaches **Time up**, your answers are handed in as they stand.

To stop early, press **End exam**. Any question you have not answered is
marked as wrong.

## See how you did

Your result is **Passed** or **Not passed**, with a breakdown of your
score.

- Press **View results by question** to see each question and your answer.
- Press **Download certificate** to keep a copy of your certificate. It is offered when you pass a module that gives one.
- Press **Try again** to sit the assessment again. It is offered straight after an attempt you did not pass, where the module allows another.

Every attempt is listed under **My history** on the **Teaching modules**
page. Choose one to open its result again.
