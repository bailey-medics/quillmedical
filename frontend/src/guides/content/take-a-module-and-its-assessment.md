# Take a module and its assessment

A module has two parts: learning materials to read at your own pace, and
an assessment. You can leave the learning materials and come back. The
assessment is sat in one go.

## Open your module

1. Sign in. You arrive at **Teaching modules**. If you are somewhere else, choose **Teaching** in the side menu.
   ![The Teaching modules page, with a card for each module](take-a-module-and-its-assessment/teaching-modules.png)
2. Press **View module** on the module you want.
   ![A module's page, with its learning materials and its assessment](take-a-module-and-its-assessment/module.png)

## Work through the learning materials

Some modules have no learning materials. If yours has none, go straight
to the assessment.

1. Press **Start learning**. The first slide opens.
2. Press **Next** to move on a slide. The arrow keys work too, and on a phone you can swipe.
   ![A slide of the learning materials, with the list of slides beside it](take-a-module-and-its-assessment/slide.png)
3. Use the list of slides in the side menu to jump to one you want to read again. **Slide progress** shows how far through you are.
4. Some slides hold a recorded lecture. Press play on the video, and move on when you are ready.
5. On the last slide, press **Finish**. You are taken back to the module.
   ![The last slide, with the Finish button](take-a-module-and-its-assessment/last-slide.png)

To stop part way, choose **Exit lesson** in the side menu. You can start
again from any slide.

## Sit the assessment

Pick a time when you will not be interrupted. Once you begin, leaving the
page hands in whatever you have answered.

The pictures here are of an example module about chest X-rays. Yours
will have its own questions.

1. Press **Start assessment**.
2. If the module has an introduction, read it, then press **Begin**.
   ![The introduction to an assessment, with the Begin button](take-a-module-and-its-assessment/introduction.png)
3. Read the question, scroll down to the answers, choose one and press **Next**. **Question progress** shows how many questions are left. You can go back and change an answer.
   ![A question in an assessment, with the time left and the End exam button above it](take-a-module-and-its-assessment/question.png)
4. On the last question, press **Submit & finish**.
5. Press **View results**.
   ![The page shown when every question is answered](take-a-module-and-its-assessment/finished.png)

If the module is timed, a countdown shows at the top of the page. When it
reaches **Time up**, your answers are handed in as they stand.

To stop early, press **End exam**. You are asked to confirm, because any
question you have not answered is marked as wrong.

![The check shown before an exam is ended early](take-a-module-and-its-assessment/end-exam.png)

## See how you did

Your result is **Passed** or **Not passed**, with a breakdown of your
score.

![A passed result, with its score and certificate](take-a-module-and-its-assessment/result.png)

- Press **View results by question** to see each question and your answer.
  ![The results by question page](take-a-module-and-its-assessment/results-by-question.png)
- Press **Download certificate** to keep a copy of your certificate. It is offered when you pass a module that gives one.
- Press **Try again** to sit the assessment again. It is offered straight after an attempt you did not pass, where the module allows another.

Every attempt is listed under **My history** on the **Teaching modules**
page. Choose one to open its result again.
