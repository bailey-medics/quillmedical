# Quill update for EoEETA, 14 October 2026

Dear all,

Below is the fourth update of the Teaching Platform work (1-14th October):

- Thank you for agreeing that I can name EoEETA and use your logo. Both are now on quill-medical.com, alongside a page about the colorectal polyps module.
- I have drafted the Terms of Service and Privacy Policy, with a section that applies to EoEETA alone. Anyone creating an account will now be shown a notice linking to both. I will upload the T&Cs and policy whilst signed off by both EoEETA and Bailey Medics.
- If someone decides to deactivate their account (or an admin at EoEETA decides to), training records are deleted three and a half years after an account is deactivated. 3.5 years was taken as you have asked for reassessment every 3 years. Nothing is deleted automatically: the platform lists what is due, and I will confirm as superadmin if to delete or not.
- Added automatic email reminders. Someone enrolled on a module who has not started it is reminded weekly for 6 weeks, and anyone whose pass is due to be repeated is told in good time.
- Made the exams more robust. An exam now finishes itself when its time runs out, even if the learner has closed the page. The countdown is checked against the server on every answer, a brief loss of connection no longer interrupts the exam, and each learner can only have one attempt in progress at a time.
- The delegates page now shows results one module at a time, with each delegate's site. Hence, you can now have more than one module at EoEETA if you so desire.
- Added a guides section (basically an FAQ), with illustrated step-by-step instructions for learners, clinical leads and administrators. The guide to joining can be read before signing in. This currently only includes placeholder text and will need to be updated to be fully informative.
- Emails from the platform are now sent through Amazon's email service, from servers in London, making the platform fully UK placed.
- Upgraded the video player. The playback controls now carry the platform's own design rather than each browser's, and every lecture shows a still image before it plays.
- Tables now turn into easy-to-read cards on narrower screens, so nothing has to be scrolled sideways on a phone or tablet.
- Continued strengthening security: every page and action is now automatically checked to confirm it asks who is using it, oversized requests are refused, and every change is scanned for leaked passwords or keys and for known vulnerabilities before it can go live.
- The learning and assessment sections now say that both are best down in a quiet place on a laptop, rather than mobile device.
