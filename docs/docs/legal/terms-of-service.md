### 8. The clinician passport: sign-offs, records and assessors

8.1 The clinician passport is a record of what you have been assessed as
able to do. It holds two kinds of record and keeps them apart.

- **Sign-offs** are made by a named assessor. A sign-off is that
  assessor's own professional act: a statement that they observed and
  judged your practice, on the evidence the sign-off names. We record who
  made it, when and on what basis, and we show that history. We do not
  check a sign-off and we do not guarantee that it is right. A sign-off
  cannot be edited; it can only be superseded by a later one, and the
  history stays.
- **Self-declared records** are entered by you: logbook entries,
  certificates and qualifications you claim, CPD entries and reflections.
  Nobody countersigns them. A logbook proves activity, not competence.

8.2 A sign-off grants nothing. Holding a sign-off does not authorise you
to carry out that procedure anywhere. Every organisation decides for
itself who may act within it, taking the passport into account alongside
its own checks.

8.3 If you sign a competency off for somebody, you confirm that you
observed and judged their practice as the sign-off describes, that you
are qualified to do so, and that you will be honest, accurate, fair and
objective, as your professional body requires. You are accountable for
your sign-off. If you later believe a sign-off you made was wrong, record
a superseding one; do not ask us to delete the history.

8.4 You may invite somebody outside Quill to sign off a competency. We
will email them an invitation on your behalf, and you must have their
agreement before you give us their email address.

8.5 The passport is yours to keep. You can download a complete copy at
